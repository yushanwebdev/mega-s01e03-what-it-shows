Two tickets

The counts above are computed from the same grouping. One ticket per cause:

  • parser — 379 reports. The shared parser reads every date month-first; eleven tenants write day-first, so a booking for 7 May becomes an invite for 5 July. App and email always use the parser. A booking-v2 form prints DD/MM/YYYY, so its input also lands here.
  • hint — 33 reports. Three tenants run booking-v3, whose form prints MM/DD/YYYY. Their people type month-first as the hint says, and the form reads it day-first. The flip goes the other way, and the parser fix does not touch it.

Why the tags cannot decide

Intake tags are guesses. In these reports, 23 parser reports carry the hint tag and 15 hint reports carry the parser tag. The cause comes from the tenant's locale and form, as causes.ts does it, not from meta.intakeTag.

The export holds 427 rows but 412 unique report IDs. uniqueById removes the overlap before grouping. Every unique report belongs to #4471, so the two tickets cover all 412.
